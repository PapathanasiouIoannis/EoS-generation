# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Everyone participating in this project is expected to help maintain a
respectful, precise, and constructive scientific community, regardless of
experience, identity, background, or career stage.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss evidence, methods, and code without attacking people.
- State assumptions and uncertainty clearly.
- Welcome questions and good-faith correction.
- Respect privacy, attribution, licenses, and publication boundaries.
- Avoid harassment, intimidation, discriminatory language, and unwanted
  personal attention.

Scientific disagreement is welcome. Persistent hostility, personal attacks,
or misrepresentation of another contributor's work are not.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate contributions and may
temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary. Decisions
will consider context, severity, recurrence, and the safety of affected
participants.

Report conduct concerns privately through the contact options on the
[maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/PapathanasiouIoannis). Do not
include sensitive personal information in a public issue. If no suitable
private contact method is available, use GitHub's reporting tools.

Anyone handling a report must protect the reporter's privacy as far as
reasonably possible and avoid conflicts of interest.
